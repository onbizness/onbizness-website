About OnBizness Group

Built on 26 years of enterprise experience. Structured to deliver without the overhead.

OnBizness Group is a boutique management consulting firm founded in 2000. We combine principal-level leadership with a curated network of senior practitioners to match the right expertise to every engagement.

The Firm

Senior judgment at every stage. The right expertise for every challenge.

OnBizness Group was founded on a straightforward premise: organizations dealing with complex transformation challenges deserve senior-level attention throughout the engagement, not just at the point of sale. That premise has guided how the firm works for 26 years.

Every engagement is led by a principal consultant who owns the client relationship, sets the strategic direction, and remains directly accountable for outcomes from the first conversation through to delivery. When an engagement calls for additional expertise, the firm draws on a curated network of experienced practitioners, each selected for what the specific work requires, not assigned from a standing bench.

The result is a model that gives clients the senior attention of a boutique practice and the right capabilities for the problem at hand, without the overhead of a large firm or the risk of being handed off to a junior team once the engagement is underway.

How We Work

The right model for the work. Not a fixed staffing formula.

Principal-Led Engagements

Every engagement is led by a senior principal with direct accountability for outcomes. The person who scopes the work is the person delivering it. For many engagements this is the right model: focused, efficient, and free of the overhead that comes with larger team structures.

Specialist Network Engagements

When an engagement requires a broader set of capabilities, OnBizness Group draws on a curated network of senior practitioners. Each specialist is selected for the engagement based on what the work requires. The principal remains the client's single point of accountability throughout.

Embedded Advisory

For organizations that need sustained strategic support, OnBizness Group operates as an embedded advisor within the client organization. This model suits complex, evolving engagements where senior judgment, institutional knowledge, and trusted relationships matter as much as the work itself.
