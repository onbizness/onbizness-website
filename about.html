About OnBizness Group

25 years of enterprise consulting. Built on practice, not theory.

OnBizness Group was founded in 1999 after senior roles at two of the world's most respected consulting and technology firms. We have been delivering measurable results for complex organizations ever since.

Julian Montealegre founded OnBizness Group in 1999 following senior roles at KPMG and IBM, where he helped build and scale PeopleSoft consulting practices across international markets. Those years taught him what enterprise consulting looks like at its best — and what it costs organizations when the gap between strategy and execution is left unmanaged.

His career began in software development and global implementation at Dun and Bradstreet Software, where he was part of the original development team for an HR and payroll platform that was subsequently implemented across nearly 20 countries spanning Latin America, Europe, Asia, Australia, and New Zealand. That combination of product development origin and international delivery experience shaped how he thinks about enterprise problems — from the inside out.

Over 25 years, OnBizness Group has delivered engagements ranging from custom software development and offshore platform innovation in the early years, to enterprise agile transformation, intelligent automation assessments, and embedded advisory work at some of the largest financial institutions in the United States. Through every phase, the approach has remained the same: understand the real problem, work alongside the people responsible for solving it, and leave something behind that keeps generating value.

Julian holds a Bachelor of Arts in Management Information Systems from the University of South Florida and maintains active certifications in project management, agile delivery, and process improvement. He serves on the Supervisory Committee at Tampa Bay Federal Credit Union, where he contributes to audit oversight, internal controls review, and regulatory compliance monitoring.
